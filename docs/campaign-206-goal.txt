Design, from scratch, brick families that a Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless can carry with a passive magnet, lower onto a structure with 16 to 40 mm of placement error, and release by yawing once the brick is seated. Read docs/requirements.md and src/brixzle/engine/gripper_spec.py. The score is green_mm: the largest error radius inside which every release of every placement (full seat, both bare edges, and the corbel's first reach, back brick and second reach) seats, with no jam, measured on a grid (the brick must self-align); then the overhang length and stack height that noisy builds reach with 16 mm of error. The minimum worth printing is a green zone of 16 mm, a stack of 3 and an overhang of 3; more of each is better, and the maintainer will trade overhang length for tolerated error. Every design must meet the hard constraints in docs/requirements.md, including a magnet target and a mass of at most 25 g with its insert. Try several genuinely different capture ideas before refining any one: the archive keeps the best design in each error band, so a new idea competes in its own band. Results are simulated, uncalibrated contact. Do not copy designs from papers or other groups.
