Design, from scratch, brick families that a Crazyflie 2.1 Brushless can carry with a passive magnet, lower onto a structure with 16 to 40 mm of placement error, and release by yawing once the brick is seated. Read docs/requirements.md and src/brixzle/engine/gripper_spec.py. The score is r90_mm: the largest error at which every placement (full seat, both bare edges, corbel reach) seats at least 90 % of the time; then the overhang length and stack height that noisy builds reach at that error. The minimum worth printing is r90 of 16 mm, a stack of 3 and an overhang of 3; more of each is better, and the maintainer will trade overhang length for tolerated error. Every design must meet the hard constraints in docs/requirements.md, including a magnet target and a mass of at most 25 g with its insert. Try several genuinely different capture ideas before refining any one: the archive keeps the best design in each error band, so a new idea competes in its own band. Results are simulated, uncalibrated contact. Do not copy designs from papers or other groups.
